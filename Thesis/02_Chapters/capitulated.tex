\section{Chapter Organisation}

In Chapter 2, the concept and functioning of artificial neural networks will be explained, as well as the activation function, which is a method used by these networks. 
The types of neural networks will be described, including both multilayer perceptrons and convolutional networks, together with the backpropagation algorithm. 
The Optdigit dataset, which will be used for training and testing the algorithms, will also be presented. 
Finally, incremental learning and its algorithm will be described. \\

In Chapter 3, the algorithm proposed by \cite{bullinaria2009} will be implemented. Subsequently, its performance will be verified, and the results obtained using the same data mentioned in the article will be analysed, in order to confirm that the algorithm’s behaviour matches that described by the author.

In Chapter 4, the process of extending the base algorithm to allow the use of more than two layers of duplicated weights will be explained, and the same training and testing data as in the base algorithm will be applied. \\

Finally, in Chapter 5, using the results obtained, a comparison between both works will be presented to determine whether there was a significant reduction in learning rates. 
Chapter 6 will then present the conclusions and future work.
