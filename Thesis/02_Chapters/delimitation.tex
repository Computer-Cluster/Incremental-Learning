\section{Delimitation}
\label{sec:delimitation}

In this research, only Multilayer Perceptron (MLP) neural networks will be used. It should be noted that this is not the only type of neural network that exists, as there are also Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs), Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs), and Radial Basis Function Networks (RBFNs) \cite{royo2021}. Furthermore, optimisation techniques such as genetic algorithms will not be addressed, limiting the study exclusively to exploring performance improvements in neural networks with more than two duplicated-weight layers within an incremental learning context. Likewise, the study is restricted to the aforementioned networks, excluding deep neural models and the use of other datasets.
