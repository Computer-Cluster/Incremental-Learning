\section{Justification}

Artificial Neural Networks (ANNs) enable machine learning and the resolution of a wide variety of complex problems. However, as mentioned previously, traditional machine learning techniques present a significant limitation: when new data blocks are incorporated, the model tends to degrade its performance and forget previously learned information \cite{bullinaria2009}. 

Although current results in the field have shown significant progress, neural networks still possess short-term memory that deteriorates over time. In contrast, human beings are capable of learning new tasks without significantly forgetting what they previously learned. While this ability is supported by the biological structure of the brain, in the computational domain it is possible to explore configurations that partially emulate such behaviour, improving the retention of acquired knowledge.

From a technical point of view, there are no restrictions preventing experimentation with new architectures or training strategies that allow ANNs to accumulate information progressively without suffering from overwriting or loss of prior knowledge. This opens the possibility of developing more efficient and sustainable models applicable in various contexts of modern artificial intelligence.

In a scenario where incremental learning is not employed, the incorporation of new data would require retraining the entire model with all available information, which is a costly process in terms of time, energy, and computational resources. In contrast, an incremental approach allows the model to be trained only with new data, significantly reducing computational and energy costs, and making its application in real environments more feasible.

At present, various tools facilitate the implementation of artificial neural networks through specialised libraries. In this research, the development environment Google Colab will be primarily used, as it provides a free infrastructure based on GPU and full compatibility with the Python language, one of the most widely used in data science and artificial intelligence.

For the model implementation, the PyTorch library will be used, as it is one of the most powerful and flexible frameworks for deep learning development. PyTorch stands out for its dynamic nature, which allows networks to be defined and modified flexibly during execution, in addition to offering clear syntax that closely resembles pure Python. These characteristics facilitate rapid experimentation, error debugging, and integration with other scientific packages. Therefore, PyTorch represents an ideal tool for the construction and evaluation of incremental learning models in this research.
